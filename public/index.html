The Constitutional Foundation & Judicial Interpretation

संविधानं गणराज्यस्य मूलसिद्धान्तान्, अधिकारांश्च संस्थापयति। यदा विधिकर्तव्यस्य संशयः समुद्भवति, तदा न्यायपालिका संविधानाशयं व्याख्याय व्यवहारेषु न्यायं प्रतिष्ठापयति। न्यायनिर्णयैः संविधानसिद्धान्ताः प्रमाणभूतम् अर्थं लभन्ते।

The Constitution establishes the principles, rights, institutions and framework of the Republic. The judiciary interprets those constitutional provisions when questions of law and constitutional meaning come before it. Through judicial decisions, constitutional principles are applied to real disputes and given authoritative meaning.

SUPREME COURT OF INDIA

From Constitutional Principle to Judicial Interpretation

The Constitution establishes the rights, freedoms, institutions and principles that govern the Republic. When questions concerning constitutional meaning or the application of law come before the Supreme Court, the Court interprets those provisions and applies them to real disputes.

Its judgments therefore form an important bridge between constitutional principle and the law as applied in practice.

Under Article 141 of the Constitution, the law declared by the Supreme Court is binding on all courts throughout the territory of India.
